Bibliographic reference:

Skoog, West, Holler, Crouch — *Fundamentals of Analytical Chemistry* (Cengage; multiple editions). Calibration and standard-preparation chapters treat quantitative analysis as comparison against known composition standards. For gas-phase work this maps to certified cylinders and controlled dilution rather than ad-hoc uncertified mixes when metrological claims are required.
