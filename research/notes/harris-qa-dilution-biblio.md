Bibliographic / curriculum reference (not a full-text fetch):

Daniel C. Harris, *Quantitative Chemical Analysis* (W. H. Freeman / Macmillan; multiple editions). Chapters on preparing standards and dilution factors formalize:

\[c_f = c_i \times \frac{V_i}{V_f}\]

for transferring a known amount of analyte into a larger final volume. For ideal gases at the same temperature and pressure, amount ∝ volume, so volume ratios replace mass/mole ratios for binary mixing of a certified mother gas with diluent.

Use together with gas-metrology standards (ISO 6142 / 6145) when claiming calibration-gas uncertainty; Harris alone is the classroom dilution algebra.
